# The first week

Sixty minutes a day. Each day has one objective, new material, revision, active recall, a listening task and a number you can read off the Dashboard afterwards.

| Day | Objective | Vocabulary (12) | Ṣarf (10) | Naḥw (10) | Quran Lab + listening (13) | Ḥifẓ (10) | Conversation (5) | Measured outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish the baseline | Placement diagnostic replaces the session (about 20 min) | Read Ṣ1 Roots and patterns | Read N1 The three kinds of word | Lab 1:1–1:2 | Assess Al-Mulk 1–6 by recitation | Greetings: phrases | Diagnostic score by area; estimated known words |
| 2 | Sort any word into ism / fiʿl / ḥarf | Reviews + 8 new words | Ṣ1 practice drill | N1 practice drill, then mastery check | Lab 1:3–1:4; "single words by ear" on Al-Fātiḥa | Al-Mulk 7–12 | Greetings: say from memory | N1 check ≥ 80% |
| 3 | Pull the root out of a word | Reviews + 8 new | Ṣ1 mastery check | N2 Definite and indefinite | Lab 1:5–1:7 | Al-Mulk 13–18 | Greetings: conversation | Ṣ1 check ≥ 80% |
| 4 | Read tanwīn and أَل as grammar | Reviews + 8 new | Read Ṣ2 The past verb; conjugator with three roots | N2 drill and check | Lab 67:1; "whole ayah by ear" on Al-Mulk | Al-Mulk 19–24 | Introductions: phrases | N2 check ≥ 80%; listening accuracy logged |
| 5 | Read the doer from a past verb | Reviews + 8 new | Ṣ2 drill and check | Read N3 The three cases | Lab 67:2 | Al-Mulk 25–30 | Introductions: say from memory | Ṣ2 check ≥ 80% |
| 6 | Name the case of a noun | Reviews + 8 new | Concept review if due; Ṣ3 reading | N3 drill and check | Lab 67:3; "rebuild the ayah" | Ar-Raḥmān 1–6; revisions due | Introductions: conversation | N3 check ≥ 80% |
| 7 | Consolidate and measure | Reviews only | Any due concept reviews | Targeted drill on the weakest tag | Retest the week's Lab ayahs as they come due; continuous listening Al-Mulk 1–5, text hidden | Ar-Raḥmān 7–12 | Reading passage 1 at stage 1 | Weekly assessment (20 questions) |

Notes

- The diagnostic comes first so that words you already recognise skip the introduction and go straight to recall and in-context testing.
- If a mastery check is failed, the next day's slot for that subject is the practice drill and a second attempt; the following lesson waits.
- If more than 30 vocabulary reviews are waiting, the app introduces no new words that day.
- By the end of the week all 30 ayahs of Al-Mulk have recall evidence, and Ar-Raḥmān has begun. New memorisation starts only after both surahs are assessed and revision holds at 85% or better.
- Take the first backup on day 7 (Settings → Download backup).
